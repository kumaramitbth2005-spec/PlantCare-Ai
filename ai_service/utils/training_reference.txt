PLANT DISEASES (बीमारियाँ)
Fungal Diseases:
Powdery Mildew - White powdery spots on leaves
Downy Mildew - Yellow patches with grayish mold
Rust - Orange-brown pustules on leaves
Leaf Spot - Brown/black spots with yellow halos
Anthracnose - Dark, sunken lesions
Blight - Rapid browning and dying of leaves
Fusarium Wilt - Yellowing and wilting
Root Rot - Decaying roots, yellowing leaves
Damping Off - Seedling collapse at soil line
Smut - Black powdery masses on grains

Bacterial Diseases:
Bacterial Leaf Spot - Water-soaked spots
Bacterial Wilt - Sudden wilting
Crown Gall - Tumor-like growths
Fire Blight - Blackened, scorched appearance
Soft Rot - Watery, soft decay

Viral Diseases:
Mosaic Virus - Mottled yellow and green patterns
Leaf Curl - Curling and distortion
Yellow Vein Mosaic - Yellow vein patterns
Ring Spot - Circular yellow rings
Stunting - Reduced growth

Curled leaves	Leaf Curl Virus	Virus
Sticky leaves, ants	Aphids	Insect
Holes in leaves	Caterpillars	Insect
Yellow mosaic pattern	Mosaic Virus	Virus
Water-soaked spots	Bacterial Spot	Bacteria
Orange pustules	Rust	Fungus

TREATMENT RECOMMENDATIONS
For Fungal Diseases:
Neem oil spray (15ml/L water)
Baking soda solution (5g/L water)
Copper fungicide
Remove affected leaves
Improve air circulation
Avoid overhead watering

For Bacterial Diseases:
Copper-based bactericide
Remove infected plants
Sterilize tools
Crop rotation
Resistant varieties

For Viral Diseases:
Remove infected plants
Control insect vectors
Use virus-free seeds
Disinfect tools
Plant resistant varieties

For Pests:
Neem oil spray
Insecticidal soap
Beneficial insects (Ladybugs)
Yellow sticky traps
Water spray for aphids
